# Privacy Policy for JobTrack

**Last Updated: April 23, 2026**

JobTrack ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our web platform ("Site").

## 1. Information We Collect

### Gmail API Data
JobTrack uses the Gmail API to scan your inbox for specifically job-related communications (applications, interview invites, offers, etc.). 
- **Scopes**: We use the `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly` scope.
- **Data Access**: The platform reads email headers (Subject, From, Date) and body text to identify job application updates.

### Server-Side Data
We store your job applications, statuses, and extracted metadata in a secure database to provide a synchronized experience across your devices.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- **Job Tracking**: To automatically populate your job application dashboard and journey visualization.
- **AI Summarization**: Email fragments are processed by AI providers (like Google Gemini) to extract specific details like company name, role, and salary. This data is not used for training models by our platform.
- **Improved UX**: To provide smart filters, timeline views, and analytics.

## 3. Data Integrity and Security

### Secure Storage
We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect the data stored in our database.

### Third-Party Disclosure
- **Google Services**: We use Google OAuth for authentication and Gmail API for synchronization.
- **AI Providers**: Specific email text is sent to the AI provider (Gemini/OpenRouter) for processing. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for their data handling.
- **No Selling of Data**: We **never** sell, rent, or trade your Gmail data or personal information to third parties.

## 4. Limited Use Disclosure
JobTrack's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Service User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## 5. Your Choices
- **Account Removal**: You can choose to delete your account and all associated data at any time via the "Settings" menu.
- **Data Wipe**: You can manually wipe all tracked application data at any time while keeping your account.

## 6. Contact
For concerns regarding this policy, please contact us via the official GitHub repository or support channels.
